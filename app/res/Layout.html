01Create a TV frame

Enter the LEDs per side. The frame replaces the current drawing; extra segments can be added afterwards.

02The physical strip

Drag the dots to where the LEDs are mounted, furniture included. The order follows the cable; the white circle is LED 0.

Select an LED to see its color zone on the right.

New segment

The first point is where the cable of this segment starts. New LEDs are added after the last existing one.

How to read it

Physical position: where the LED is mounted. Color zone: which part of the picture lights it. An LED under a TV stand can still follow the bottom edge of the picture.

The colored preview only helps reading the zones; it is not the live video. Editing does not change the lights until you press “Apply to TV”.

03The color zone

Click a zone to select its LED. Drag it to move the part of the picture it takes its color from.

Picture area (%)

Overlapping zones are allowed: several LEDs can follow the same part of the picture.

Exact position and order

Vertically, 100% is the bottom edge of the TV; 120% is below it.
