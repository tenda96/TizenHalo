Hi! TizenHalo is working on your TV, but the current capture method is slow (~2 fps). This version
finds the fastest one. It's a one-button FPS benchmark: it times every capture method and resolution
and writes a single file for you to send back. It does NOT drive the LEDs and nothing runs until you
press the button.

1. Install TizenHalo-Capture-Probe-0.5.0-tizen50.tpk with Apps2Samsung (same per-TV signing/install
   as before; you can replace the old probe).
2. Open "TizenHalo Capture Probe" and leave its screen open.
3. From your phone/computer on the same network, open:
   http://YOUR-TV-IP:8795/
4. Press "Run FPS benchmark". It runs for a few seconds (the screen may flicker). A file
   tizenhalo-fps-benchmark.json downloads on its own.
5. Send that JSON back, with your exact model and firmware version.
6. Back on the TV closes the app.

It measures the speed of the in-memory capture methods (secvideo) and the screenshot method (PNG and
JPEG, at two sizes). From the numbers I'll pick the fastest one that actually captures the picture, and
build a faster version of the app for you to try. If anything fails, send the error too.
