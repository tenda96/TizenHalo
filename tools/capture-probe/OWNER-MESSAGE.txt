Hi! This is the next step for your UN43RU7100FXZC / T-MSLAKUC-1500.5.

The read-only diagnostic you ran told us which capture library your TV has. This new app actually
tries to capture a few frames with it, so we can see whether a real picture comes back. It does NOT
drive the LEDs, change any TV setting, or capture anything until you press a button.

1. Install TizenHalo-Capture-Probe-0.1.0-tizen50.tpk with Apps2Samsung, using its normal per-TV
   signing/install process. It can be installed alongside the other TizenHalo apps.
2. Put a bright, colourful picture on the TV first - a still photo or a paused frame is ideal.
   Avoid a black screen, a menu or a thumbnail.
3. Open "TizenHalo Capture Probe" and leave its screen open.
4. From your phone or computer on the same network, open:
   http://YOUR-TV-IP:8795/
5. Press "Run capture test". A report file downloads on its own. Send that JSON back, and tell us:
   - your exact model and firmware version,
   - what was on screen during the test (which app/source, roughly which colours).
6. Back on the TV closes the app and stops its local server.

It makes three capture calls at 640x360, one after another. If installation is refused, or the app
closes before showing an address, send the exact error or describe what you saw. A failure is still
a useful result - please send it too.

One thing to know: this is the first time this capture path is tried on a TV like yours, so we do
not yet know if it returns a usable picture, the right colours, or anything at all. That is exactly
what this test is for. Only after a successful capture will we build an Ambilight version for your TV.
