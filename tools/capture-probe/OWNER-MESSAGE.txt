Hi again! Good news first: the earlier test proved your TV does return captured frames. The reason it
looked black was just that nothing coloured was on the video plane. This version fixes that in a way
that needs nothing from you.

What's new: the app now PAINTS full-screen red, green and blue on the TV itself and captures each one.
So there's no photo to load and nothing to keep open in the background - it does everything on its own.
It still does NOT drive the LEDs or change any TV setting, and nothing runs until you press a button.

1. Install TizenHalo-Capture-Probe-0.3.0-tizen50.tpk with Apps2Samsung (same per-TV signing/install
   as before; you can replace the old one).
2. Open "TizenHalo Capture Probe" and leave its screen open.
3. From your phone/computer on the same network, open:
   http://YOUR-TV-IP:8795/
4. Press "Run capture test". The TV will briefly flash red, green and blue - that's the test painting
   the colours it captures. A report file downloads on its own.
5. Send that JSON back, and tell me your exact model and firmware version.
6. Back on the TV closes the app.

That's it. If anything fails, send the error or describe what you saw - a failure is still useful.
