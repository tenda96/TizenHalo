Hi again! The first capture test worked - the TV did return frames. But the video plane was dark
(nothing was playing), so we could not see any colour yet. This updated version fixes that.

What changed: it now captures BOTH the video plane AND the whole screen, and it samples the picture
across a grid instead of just the centre. So this time a solid-colour still image is enough. It still
does NOT drive the LEDs, change any TV setting, or capture anything until you press a button.

1. Install TizenHalo-Capture-Probe-0.2.0-tizen50.tpk with Apps2Samsung (same per-TV signing/install
   as before; you can replace the old one).
2. Put a BRIGHT, SOLID-COLOUR picture full screen - a plain red or blue image or video is ideal.
   A photo works now too.
3. Open "TizenHalo Capture Probe" and leave its screen open.
4. From your phone/computer on the same network, open:
   http://YOUR-TV-IP:8795/
5. Press "Run capture test". A report downloads on its own. Send that JSON back, and tell us:
   - your exact model and firmware version,
   - roughly what colour filled the screen during the test.
6. Back on the TV closes the app and stops its server.

If anything fails, send the error or describe what you saw - a failure is still useful. Thanks a lot!
