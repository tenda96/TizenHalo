Hi! I've prepared a small diagnostic for your UN43RU7100FXZC / T-MSLAKUC-1500.5.

Samsung lists the RU7100 as Tizen 5.0, while the current TizenHalo package targets
5.5. This diagnostic is compiled specifically for 5.0. It does not capture video,
change TV settings or control the LEDs. Its startup on your TV is still untested.

1. Install TizenHalo-Compatibility-Probe-0.1.0-tizen50.tpk with Apps2Samsung,
   using its normal per-TV signing/install process.
2. Open "TizenHalo Compatibility Probe" and leave it open.
3. Open the address shown on the TV from your phone/computer on the same network:
   http://YOUR-TV-IP:8794/
4. Download the compatibility report and send the JSON back, together with your
   exact model and firmware version.
5. If possible, also download the listed libvideo-capture, libsec-video-capture,
   libvideoenhance, libavoc and libdisplay-capture-api libraries. Send these files
   privately in a ZIP, not in a public issue. Some links may be absent or denied
   by the TV sandbox; tell us if that happens. Identical hashes identify aliases,
   so only one copy of each identical library is needed.
6. Back on the TV closes the app and stops the local diagnostic server.

If installation is refused or the diagnostic closes before showing an address,
send the exact installation error or describe what appeared on screen.

Why this helps: the official firmware is encrypted with the MuseL family key,
which is not covered by the public candidate set tested so far. Reading the
capture libraries from your running TV lets us inspect their actual ABI without
root or changing firmware. Only then can we build a capture adapter for your TV.
